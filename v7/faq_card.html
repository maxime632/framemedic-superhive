RENDERS AND FILES

Will Frame Medic change my renders?

No. By default, everything Relief changes steps aside for F12, Ctrl F12 and Render-menu renders, then comes back. A render keeps Relief only if you choose so.

Will it change my .blend files?

Relief is lifted before every save and put back right after, so your file stays the scene you built and colleagues without Frame Medic get full quality. A fix you click yourself is a real edit, like one you would make by hand, and you can undo it from Changes Made.

Does it write anything to disk?

Only Ready Frames, and only when you turn them on. They go to Blender's temporary folder (8 GB at most by default), or next to your .blend if you choose Keep With the File. Frame Medic writes no backup copies.

RIGS, SIMULATIONS AND SCATTERS

Can it break my character rigs?

Deformers, mesh binds, constraint targets and driven values are never paused or changed, by Relief or by a fix. It works with Rigify, CloudRig and other rigs, and sets a rig's own detail switch to its lightest while you work.

Will it stop my simulations?

No. A cloth, soft body or simulation nodes computed live keep playing, and baked simulations and caches always play as they are. Frame Medic lightens what comes after them.

Does it work with scatter add-ons?

Yes. It recognises Geo-Scatter, OpenScatter, engon, BagaPie and GScatter and never hides, pauses or merges their scatters. Geo-Scatter scatters can be drawn lighter through Geo-Scatter's own Display As, in one click.

Is it just Simplify?

Simplify is one switch for the whole scene, and it changes renders if you forget it. Frame Medic works object by object, measures before and after, also covers textures in EEVEE, rigs, drivers, Grease Pencil and add-on scripts, and always gives the scene back.

Does it help with Cycles?

Yes, in the viewport: fewer viewport samples, the denoiser, and half resolution if you want it. Cycles final renders are untouched.

YOUR COMPUTER

Which Blender versions and systems?

Blender 4.0 to 5.2 on Windows, macOS (Apple silicon included) and Linux. You get the extension package for 4.2 and later, and the add-on package for 4.0 and 4.1.

Will it help on a laptop with a weak GPU?

That is where it helps the most. Set Machine to Modest in the preferences.

Does it connect to the internet?

No. There is no account and no telemetry.

LICENSES AND HELP

Which license do I need?

Personal if you work alone and nobody pays for the work. Pro if you work alone on paid work. Studio for a team of up to ten in one company or school. Same features in all three.

Can I install it on my laptop and my desktop?

Yes. Personal and Pro cover up to three computers that you use yourself.

Every 1.x update is included with every license.
