01HEAL THIS SCENE

One click runs the whole checkup

1

It scans the scene and ranks every slowdown by its cost per frame.

2

It applies the fixes that keep your renders identical, and turns Relief on in Solid, Material Preview and Rendered.

3

It plays real frames and keeps a stronger setting only when playback gets faster.

On Singularity 100_0040 it found 140 slowdowns and fixed 12 in 6 seconds. The health of the scene went from 2 to 100.

02IT TELLS YOU WHY

Every slowdown comes with its reason

The checkup knows about a hundred kinds of slowdown, from subdivision computed on the CPU and booleans rebuilt at every frame to 8K textures filling video memory or a second graphics card sitting idle. Each one is explained in plain words. About seventy have a one-click fix, and you can undo every fix.

03RELIEF

A lighter viewport while you work

Relief lightens the viewport object by object: subdivision above a level, textures when video memory runs short, Material Preview, hair, Grease Pencil effects, and the detail switch of rigs that have one. While the animation plays, heavy rigs stop drawing their bones and steady drivers freeze. Pause Relief and the scene is exactly as you made it.

04NEW IN 1.1

READY FRAMES

Turn it on and Frame Medic computes the heaviest objects of every frame while Blender is idle, then plays them from memory in your viewport. You can still orbit and zoom.

Blender Studio's 020_0030 (2,475 objects) plays at 20 to 22 fps instead of 1.5 on a MacBook Pro M3.

CRASH SHIELD

A Remesh voxel size typed too small or a Subdivision level dragged too high can freeze Blender before you can undo. Crash Shield sets the largest value that fits before Blender computes it, and a card tells you. Allow sets the value you asked for.

05YOUR WORK

What stays exactly as you built it

✓

Final renders. Relief steps aside for F12, Ctrl F12 and Render-menu renders, then comes back. A render keeps Relief only if you ask.

✓

Saved files. Relief is lifted before every save and put back after, so colleagues and render farms without the add-on get the full scene.

✓

Rigs. Deformers, mesh binds, constraint targets and driven values are never paused or changed.

✓

Simulations. A cloth, soft body or simulation computed live keeps playing, and baked caches are read as they are.

✓

Privacy. No internet connection, no account, no telemetry.
